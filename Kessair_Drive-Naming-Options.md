## The problem with the current names

```
K·CORP·00 · Governance & Capital      ← cryptic · dots, a code, AND a number to decode
```

Goals for a better name: **group by company · sort in *your* order · read instantly · easy to type.**

Company order used below (your workflow order, not A–Z): **1 HQ · 2 Acquisitions · 3 Properties · 4 Homes · 5 Development · 6 Equity.**

## Quick decision guide

| You want… | Pick |
|---|---|
| Clean order + plain words | **A** ⭐ |
| Fastest visual scan / icons | **B** (or A+B blend) |
| Pure readability, order doesn't matter | **C** |
| Least disruption | **D** |

> **My pick: Option A**, optionally with Option B's emoji in front (`2.1 📇 Acquisitions — Lead Data`) — you get correct ordering *and* instant visual ID, with no cryptic codes.

**Two toggles that apply to any option:**
- **Keep "Kessair" or drop it?** Since every drive is Kessair, you can drop it on the operating companies (just `Acquisitions`) and keep it only on `Kessair HQ` — or keep it everywhere for brand consistency.
- **Separator:** I used `—` (em dash). `-` (hyphen) or `/` also work — your call.
